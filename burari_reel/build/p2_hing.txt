Yeh sab shuru hua do hazaar saat mein, jab parivaar ke mukhiya, Lalit ke pita, guzar gaye. Uske baad Lalit ne daava kiya ki pita ki aatma usmein aati hai aur usse baat karti hai. Wahi aadesh deti hai. Aur dheere-dheere... poora parivaar woh aadesh maanne laga. Dukaan chali. Paisa aaya. Yaqeen aur gehra hua. Phir aakhri aadesh aaya: bargad ki tapasya... bargad ki jataon ki tarah latakna.
Delhi police aur CBI ki psychological autopsy ne ise shared psychosis kaha... jab ek insaan ka bhram, poore parivaar ka sach ban jaata hai. CBI ne ise aatmahatya nahin, ek ritual ke dauraan hua haadsa bataaya. Koi tantrik ya baba nahin mila.
Lekin kuch sawaal aaj bhi wahin latke hain. Jo parivaar marne se das din pehle ek shaandaar sagaai mana raha tha, woh itna bada faisla kaise le sakta tha? Aur kuch rishtedaar aaj bhi kehte hain, ki yeh qatl tha.
Sabse daraavni baat yeh nahin ki gyaarah log mar gaye. Sabse daraavni baat yeh hai ki woh apne hi ghar mein, apnon ke beech, ek aise yaqeen ke saath jee rahe the... jise kisi ne kabhi sawaal nahin kiya.
Aapko kya lagta hai... yeh shared psychosis tha, ya kuch aur? Comment mein bataaiye. Aur aisi ansuljhi kahaaniyon ke liye follow karein.
