11 log... ek raat... aur ghar ka darwaza andar se khula tha.
1 July 2018. Subah ke sawa saat baje. Burari ka ek padosi, Gurcharan Singh, roz ki tarah apne dost Lalit ke saath morning walk par jaane aaya. Lekin Lalit aaj nahin aaya. Dukaan bhi band thi. Usne darwaza dekha... khula tha. Woh seedhiyan chadha... aur jo usne dekha, usne uski rooh kampa di.
Chhat ki lohe ki jaali se das log latke the. Aankhon par patti. Munh par tape. Kaanon mein rui. Aur haath bandhe hue. Doosre kamre mein assi saal ki daadi, Narayani Devi, zameen par padi thin. Poore ghar mein sirf ek hi zinda tha... unka kutta, Jacky, jo chhat par bandha hua tha. Sabse chhote sirf pandrah saal ke the.
Police ko laga, yeh qatl hai. Rishtedaar bhi yahi keh rahe the. Lekin phir saamne waale ghar ka CCTV khula. Raat das baje: paanch stool ghar mein le jaaye ja rahe hain. Das bajkar pandrah minute: do kishore dukaan se bijli ke taar la rahe hain. Das bajkar chaalis minute: khaana aaya, rotiyan. Raat gyaarah baje: Bhuvnesh kutte ko tehla raha hai. Aur phir... raat bhar koi baahar se andar nahin aaya. Koi nahin.
Ghar ke mandir ke paas mileen gyaarah diaries. Saal do hazaar saat se, marne ki raat se pehle tak. Aur unmein likha tha... sab kuch. Kaun kahaan khada hoga. Munh par tape kaise lagega. Haath kaise bandhenge. Kis kram mein latakna hai. Jo police ko mila, woh hoobahoo waisa hi tha, jaisa likha tha.
Par sabse daraavni baat... in diaries mein yeh bhi likha tha ki rassiyan bandhne ke baad sab ek-doosre ke haath khol denge. Unhein laga tha... woh zinda bach jaayenge.
